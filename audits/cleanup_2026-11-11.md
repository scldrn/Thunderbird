Several preparation scripts generate these archives again. A size-grouped SHA-256 scan identified 9.65 GiB of logical duplicate data in files of at least 1 MiB, much of it meshes and fields in distinct CFD cases. Those paths were retained because case integrity, historical evidence and paused-solver state were not established as disposable. Logical duplicate size is not necessarily disk space recoverable on APFS. The temporary 6 MiB partial recovery inventory generated during this session was also removed; the smaller Markdown inventory and review remain.

`AGENTS.md` was reduced to operational instructions; the research and cover-study summary moved to `docs/research_context.md`, which should be read on demand. The cleanup does not claim that all old or irrelevant research files were identified. Source Markdown was previously compared byte-for-byte, but iCloud later evicted many source files and a repeat comparison could not read those placeholders. Source download requests already made to macOS may continue independently of the stopped copier.

After the user changed the scope to an essential working tree, a path inventory found 191 source files absent from the checkout, excluding the intentionally removed caches, frame directories, Finder metadata and ten archives. They include CAD, JSON audit data, scripts and CFD artifacts; their importance has not been fully adjudicated. Do not interpret their absence as a decision that each is obsolete. The earlier Markdown-only inventory is a historical pre-recovery snapshot, not the current checkout state.
